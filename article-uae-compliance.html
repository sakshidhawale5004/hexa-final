UAE Transfer Pricing Compliance Updates

📅 April 10, 2024 ⏱️ 8 min read ✍️ HexaTP Team

The UAE has recently updated its transfer pricing regulations and compliance requirements. This article provides an overview of the latest updates and what businesses operating in the UAE need to know.

UAE Transfer Pricing Regulatory Framework

The UAE has strengthened its transfer pricing regulatory framework to align with international best practices and OECD guidelines. These updates reflect the UAE's commitment to ensuring fair taxation and preventing base erosion and profit shifting.

Important: The UAE's updated transfer pricing regulations apply to all multinational enterprises with related party transactions in the UAE.

Key Updates to UAE Transfer Pricing Rules

1. Enhanced Documentation Requirements

The UAE now requires more comprehensive transfer pricing documentation, including:

  • Detailed functional analysis of related party transactions
  • Comprehensive economic analysis
  • Robust benchmarking studies
  • Clear documentation of transfer pricing method selection
  • Annual updates to documentation

2. Contemporaneous Documentation

Documentation must be prepared contemporaneously with transactions. This is critical for:

  • Penalty protection
  • Demonstrating good faith compliance
  • Supporting audit defense

3. Transfer Pricing Study Requirements

The UAE requires transfer pricing studies for significant related party transactions. These studies must include:

  • Identification of comparable transactions
  • Analysis of comparability factors
  • Determination of appropriate transfer prices
  • Documentation of methodology and assumptions

4. Increased Audit Activity

The UAE tax authorities have increased transfer pricing audit activity, focusing on:

  • Adequacy of transfer pricing documentation
  • Arm's length nature of transfer prices
  • Compliance with transfer pricing regulations

Compliance Obligations for Businesses

Documentation Preparation

Businesses must prepare comprehensive transfer pricing documentation that includes:

  • Master file with overview of business and transfer pricing policies
  • Local files with detailed transaction-specific documentation
  • Benchmarking studies supporting transfer prices

Filing Requirements

Businesses must file transfer pricing documentation with their tax returns or upon request by tax authorities. Key requirements include:

  • Timely filing of documentation
  • Complete and accurate information
  • Supporting evidence and analysis

Record Keeping

Businesses must maintain comprehensive records related to transfer pricing, including:

  • Contracts and agreements
  • Financial data and analysis
  • Benchmarking studies
  • Correspondence with related parties

Penalties for Non-Compliance

Documentation Penalties

Failure to maintain adequate transfer pricing documentation can result in:

  • Penalties ranging from 5% to 50% of the adjustment
  • Interest on unpaid taxes
  • Potential criminal prosecution for serious violations

Transfer Pricing Adjustments

If transfer prices are found to be non-compliant, tax authorities may:

  • Adjust transfer prices to arm's length levels
  • Assess additional taxes and penalties
  • Impose interest on unpaid taxes

Best Practices for UAE Compliance

1. Conduct Transfer Pricing Study

Conduct a comprehensive transfer pricing study for all significant related party transactions. This provides the foundation for compliance.

2. Prepare Contemporaneous Documentation

Prepare transfer pricing documentation contemporaneously with transactions. This demonstrates good faith compliance and provides stronger evidence.

3. Maintain Organized Records

Maintain well-organized records that are easily accessible during audits. Use consistent naming conventions and clear file structures.

4. Monitor Regulatory Changes

Stay informed about changes to UAE transfer pricing regulations and tax authority positions. Subscribe to industry publications and attend relevant seminars.

5. Engage Specialists

Engage transfer pricing specialists with UAE expertise to ensure compliance with local regulations and best practices.

Recommendation: Conduct a transfer pricing compliance review to assess your current position and identify any gaps that need to be addressed in light of the updated UAE regulations.

Conclusion

The UAE's updated transfer pricing regulations represent a significant strengthening of the regulatory framework. Businesses operating in the UAE must ensure compliance with these updated requirements to avoid penalties and audit adjustments. By following best practices and engaging qualified specialists, businesses can navigate the UAE's transfer pricing landscape successfully.
