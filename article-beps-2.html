BEPS 2.0 Implementation Timeline Announced

April 25, 2024 8 min read HexaTP Team

The OECD has announced the implementation timeline for BEPS 2.0 initiatives. This article provides an overview of the timeline and what multinational enterprises need to do to prepare.

BEPS 2.0 Overview

BEPS 2.0 represents the next phase of the OECD's efforts to address base erosion and profit shifting. It includes two main pillars that will significantly impact global tax policy.

Important: BEPS 2.0 will fundamentally change how multinational enterprises are taxed globally. Early preparation is essential.

Pillar One: Reallocation of Taxing Rights

Overview

Pillar One reallocates taxing rights for large multinational enterprises, particularly those in the digital economy. Key features include:

  • Applies to companies with global revenue exceeding €20 billion
  • Reallocates 25% of profits above 10% profit margin
  • Affects digital and non-digital businesses

Implementation Timeline

  • 2024: Finalization of rules and guidance
  • 2025: Pilot phase begins
  • 2026: Full implementation expected

Pillar Two: Global Minimum Tax

Overview

Pillar Two introduces a global minimum tax of 15% on large corporations. Key features include:

  • Applies to companies with global revenue exceeding €750 million
  • Minimum tax rate of 15%
  • Includes both income inclusion rule and undertaxed payments rule

Implementation Timeline

  • 2024: Model rules finalized
  • 2025: Jurisdictions begin implementation
  • 2026: Widespread implementation expected

Impact on Transfer Pricing

Enhanced Documentation Requirements

BEPS 2.0 will require enhanced transfer pricing documentation, including:

  • More detailed functional analysis
  • Comprehensive economic analysis
  • Robust benchmarking studies
  • Annual documentation updates

Transfer Pricing Method Changes

BEPS 2.0 may influence transfer pricing method selection and application:

  • Greater emphasis on profit allocation methods
  • Enhanced scrutiny of intangible asset transfers
  • Focus on substance-over-form analysis

Preparation Steps for Multinational Enterprises

Immediate Actions (2024)

  • Assess your company's exposure to BEPS 2.0
  • Review current transfer pricing policies
  • Evaluate impact on tax planning strategies
  • Engage transfer pricing specialists

Short-term Actions (2025)

  • Update transfer pricing documentation
  • Implement necessary policy changes
  • Prepare for pilot phase requirements
  • Monitor regulatory developments

Long-term Actions (2026+)

  • Ensure full compliance with BEPS 2.0 requirements
  • Maintain contemporaneous documentation
  • Monitor regulatory changes and guidance
  • Adjust tax planning strategies as needed

Key Considerations

1. Profit Allocation

BEPS 2.0 will change how profits are allocated among jurisdictions. Companies need to reassess their profit allocation strategies.

2. Effective Tax Rate

The global minimum tax of 15% will affect companies with effective tax rates below this threshold. Review your current effective tax rate.

3. Transfer Pricing Policies

Transfer pricing policies may need to be adjusted to comply with BEPS 2.0 requirements and to optimize tax efficiency within the new framework.

4. Documentation

Enhanced documentation requirements will increase compliance burdens. Invest in systems and processes to manage documentation efficiently.

Recommendation: Conduct a comprehensive BEPS 2.0 impact assessment to understand how these initiatives will affect your organization and develop a preparation plan.

Conclusion

BEPS 2.0 represents a significant shift in global tax policy. Multinational enterprises must begin preparing now to ensure compliance and optimize their tax positions within the new framework. Early action will help minimize disruption and ensure a smooth transition.
